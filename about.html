About

This is the other desk — a page over from the research site, where the notebook margins live instead of the results. My work has me looking at galaxy filaments and large-scale structure most days; this corner is for the rest of it — philosophy, travel notes, half-formed thoughts that don't belong in an abstract.

The look borrows from Raphael's School of Athens — a room full of people who disagreed with each other constructively, under one roof, without needing to resolve it. That's roughly the spirit I'd like this space to keep: curious, a little unfinished, and not in a hurry to arrive anywhere in particular.

Nothing here is final. Consider it a place to think out loud — updated whenever there's something worth saying, and rewritten whenever there isn't yet.
